Voice and video call panel: participant tiles, status line and controls.

The panel is always dark in Light and Dark, because video reads best on black. Luna gives it the blue glass look. Provide the tiles (a green ring on the speaker; an avatar when the camera is off), the status (the protection state plus the duration) and the Mute, Camera, Share and Leave controls. Agents can join as transcribers. They show in the status line, never as a video tile.
