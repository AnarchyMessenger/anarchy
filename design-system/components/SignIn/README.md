Split-screen sign-up and onboarding inside the frame: the form on a panel on the left (46%), the frame's gradient on the right carrying the person's identity card, which fills in live as they go.

Steps, one question each, with a four-segment progress mark: server → account (outline "Continue with Google" or "Continue with <Org> SSO", an "or" rule, email → 6-digit code that submits itself; under a rule, quiet rows for "Continue anonymously" and "I have a guest invite") → what it's for (Work, Freelance, Personal, Community) → lock this device (passphrase, strength bar, confirm; skippable only where a keychain protects the key) → your card (name, username + tag, picture, colour). Primary buttons are `ink`, not `primary`. Errors sit under the form in `danger`. Under 900px the art side is hidden.
