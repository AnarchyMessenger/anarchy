A settings page: title, one sentence saying what the page is for, then one of three row types.

- **Facts:** label on the left in `ink-muted`, value on the right (Account).
- **Toggles:** checkbox, bold label, one-line explanation; dependent toggles dim when their parent is off (Notifications).
- **List rows:** identifier in `mono`, date underneath, and on the right a tag ("This computer", "Revoked") or one outline button ("Revoke") (Devices).

Destructive actions say what happens next in plain words ("it's signed out and removed from your channels"). No confirmation dialog for reversible actions.
