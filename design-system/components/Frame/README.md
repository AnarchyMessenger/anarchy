The window frame: a three-stop gradient around a neutral floating panel, the way Arc does it.

Ten choices: Ember (default), Cobalt, Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter, Coral, Ocean, Forest, Dusk, from the `frame-<name>-a/b/c` tokens (135°, a → b at 55% → c). The panel is inset 8px, `radius-lg`, with a 1px stroke *outside* its edge at 8% black (7% white in Dark) plus `shadow-2`. Only the rail's icons and labels sit on the frame. Dark lays a 60% `canvas` wash over it. Luna ignores the choice and uses its own blues. Gradients never appear anywhere else.
